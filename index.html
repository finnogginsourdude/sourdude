About us

Meet Katie, aka the Sour-Dude

Hi, I'm Katie Squires. What started as a jar of starter on the counter turned into a weekly habit, then a few loaves for friends, and now Fin Noggin Sour-Dude.

Every loaf is naturally leavened and fermented slowly, so it takes a couple of days from mixing to baking. No commercial yeast and no shortcuts. Just flour, water, salt, and time.

Good bread takes its time. Kind of like a sea turtle.
